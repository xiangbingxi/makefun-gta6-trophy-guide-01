      About the data

      Names, descriptions and grades come from the official PlayStation 5 list as published by PSNProfiles and Exophase, cross-checked against the guides below. Rockstar and Sony had not published the list officially as of 20 November 2026, so some details may still be refined. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have no verified unlock method yet — their entries admit that instead of guessing. Fair Game’s exact animal is still being confirmed. Every guide here is written in our own words; the sources beside each one are where its facts come from.
